Each local theme contains the original Move.mp3, Capture.mp3 and Check.mp3
from its source directory. No audio has been edited or re-encoded.
The corresponding upstream Checkmate.mp3 is a symlink to Check.mp3;
Chessism resolves that alias in its sound catalog. Castling uses Move.mp3.
