Adaptations: mono, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbit/s MP3; short fade-out on each
recording. No voices, synthesized tones, or recordings from chess sites.

move.mp3: light impact, faded over the last 40 ms.
capture.mp3: medium impact, faded over the last 50 ms.
castle.mp3: two light impacts, the second delayed 110 ms and at 85% gain.
check.mp3: light impact at 60% gain followed by a medium impact after 75 ms.
mate.mp3: two heavy impacts, the second delayed 140 ms and at 80% gain.
The paired impacts use a non-normalizing mix with a peak limiter at 0.9.
